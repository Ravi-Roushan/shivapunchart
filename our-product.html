3D Emboss

3D Emboss dies are precision tooling solutions used to create multi-level, sculpted and highly detailed raised effects on paper, board, packaging and premium print products.

Laser Dies

Laser dies are high-precision cutting tools used for paper, cardboard, PVC sheets, rigid boxes and foam, delivering accurate and consistent die-cutting performance.

Pre Rubber Dies

Pre-rubber dies are specialized steel-rule dies with pre-applied rubber ejection material, designed for fast, clean and accurate high-volume cutting.

Punch + Emboss Online

Punch + Emboss Online tooling combines punching and embossing operations for accurate registration, consistent forming and efficient packaging production.

Label Dies

Label dies are precision cutting tools designed for labels, stickers and self-adhesive materials, providing clean edges and reliable repeatability.

Sandwich Dies

Sandwich dies are multi-layer tooling solutions designed to combine die-cutting and forming operations with accurate alignment and consistent production results.

Stripping Die Male Female

Stripping die male and female sets remove waste material efficiently after die-cutting, improving production speed, accuracy and finished-product quality.

Blanking Die Male Female

Blanking die male and female tooling separates finished pieces cleanly from the sheet, supporting precise high-speed production for packaging and printed products.

Creasing Pertinex

Creasing Pertinex counter tooling provides accurate crease formation and consistent folding performance for cartons and packaging applications.

Creasing Counter Plate

Creasing counter plates are precision-made counter tools that support clean, controlled creasing and reliable folding of packaging materials.

Elecro Etg Foil Block

Electro-etched foil blocks are precision-etched tools used for detailed hot-foil stamping, delivering fine lines, sharp details and consistent decorative results.

Mag Etg Foil Block

Magnesium etched foil blocks are lightweight precision blocks designed for efficient hot-foil stamping with crisp and detailed impressions.

Texter Foil Block

Textured foil blocks create controlled surface patterns and decorative foil effects, adding premium tactile and visual finishes to printed products.

CNC Foil Block

CNC foil blocks are precision-machined tooling solutions for accurate hot-foil stamping, producing sharp details and repeatable premium finishes.

Foil + Emboss One Shot

Foil + Emboss One Shot tooling combines foil stamping and embossing in a single operation for efficient production and premium dimensional finishes.

Emboss Dies

Emboss dies are precision tools used to create raised or recessed designs on paper, board and packaging, producing crisp dimensional branding effects.

Micro Effect Dies

Micro Effect Dies create extremely fine surface details and controlled micro-patterns for security, decorative and premium packaging applications.

Step Emboss

Step emboss dies create graduated or multi-level embossing effects, producing dimensional graphics, layered textures and premium tactile finishes.

Braille Emboss

Braille emboss dies create raised tactile Braille dots on packaging and printed materials, providing clear, durable and readable accessibility markings.
